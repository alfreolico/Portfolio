Approach

Engineering, processes and web development within the same way of thinking.

I am a web developer with a mechanical engineering background and professional experience in technical environments, documentation, teaching, processes and working with people.

Engineering taught me to observe systems, work with constraints and look for clear solutions. That mindset remains present when I build web applications, interfaces, dashboards and platforms.

Integrated trajectory

  1. Mechanical engineering
  2. Processes and documentation
  3. Technical problem-solving
  4. Web development
  5. Functional products

02 · Process

How I approach a problem

Before choosing a tool, I review the need, context and constraints. The machine represents that judgement; the text explains the process.

01 · Input

Understand the problem

I identify the need before choosing a tool. The goal is to understand what the solution needs to solve and for whom.

02 · Control

Review constraints and data

I map users, information sources, permissions, available content and project limits before committing to a direction.

03 · Flow

Design a structure

I split the system into understandable screens, components and flows so information can be reviewed and evolved.

04 · Transmission

Prototype and implement

I turn the structure into a functional, reviewable interface, connecting components and data according to the defined scope.

05 · Inspection

Test and document

I verify behaviour, record relevant decisions and iterate from findings rather than a feeling that the work is finished.

06 · Return

Maintain and evolve

A solution can continue through maintenance, corrections and staged improvements when a clear new scope exists.

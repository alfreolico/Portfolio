Professional profile

Engineering, processes, and software in one profile

I am a web developer with a mechanical engineering background and professional experience in technical environments, documentation, teaching, processes, and working with people.

Background

A technical transition with context

My mechanical engineering background taught me to observe systems, work with constraints, and turn technical requirements into clear solutions. This way of thinking is still part of my work as a web developer.

My professional path includes field engineering, technical teaching, coordination, purchasing, and working with people in technical and commercial environments. These experiences strengthened my communication, organization, documentation, and ability to understand needs before proposing a solution.

Today, I bring this learning into web applications and platforms. I have built personal applications with React, JavaScript, and public APIs, as well as the multipage portfolio you are viewing.

I also take part in projects in development: an industrial monitoring web platform, where I work on frontend, backend, and dashboard features based on data exposed by the system's technical integration; a webinar registration landing page with Google Analytics; and a digital card for professional services. My main interests are applications, data, backend, dashboards, and functional interfaces.

Working approach

Build with context, not assumptions

  • Understand the problem, constraints, and data sources before writing code.
  • Break systems into components and flows that can be reviewed and improved.
  • Prototype, test, and iterate to check that the solution works in practice.
  • Document relevant decisions to keep the development process clear.

AI in my process

Move faster without giving up responsibility

I use artificial intelligence tools to research, prototype, review, and speed up implementations. Human validation of the architecture, code, and final behavior remains my responsibility.

I am open to web developer opportunities, remote work, technical collaborations, and freelance projects with a defined scope.

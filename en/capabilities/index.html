How I work

Build with context before assumptions

  1. Understand the problem

    I identify the need before choosing a tool.

  2. Review constraints and data

    I locate users, information sources, and project limits.

  3. Define scope

    I separate what is essential from what can evolve later.

  4. Design a structure

    I divide the system into understandable screens, components, and flows.

  5. Prototype and implement

    I turn the structure into a functional, reviewable interface.

  6. Test and document

    I verify behavior, record decisions, and iterate from findings.
